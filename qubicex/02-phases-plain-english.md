# The phases, in plain English

## Normal AI vs this

Normal AI hires a student and teaches them math. Aigarth breeds better
brains, then lets the best brains design the next generation.

## The hard questions, answered

**Why not just find the optimal way to learn once?**
Nobody knows if a universal best way exists, and tasks keep changing. A
structure optimal for addition may be useless for candle direction. The
bet: evolve architectures per problem and let generality emerge, instead
of humans guessing one true architecture up front.

**Is it just giving problems and storing solutions?**
Yes — minus mysticism. Each epoch poses a problem; thousands of candidate
networks attempt it; winners persist as weight sets plus structures,
hashed and archived. The archive of winning genomes is the retained
knowledge.

**How is intelligence retained?**
Three layers: (1) winning weight-sets persist in the archive — frozen as
data; (2) winning structural motifs get mutated forward instead of
reinvented — inherited like DNA; (3) ant-colony upgrades make trails
cumulative across epochs instead of restarting weekly. Teacher recursion
retains one level up: keeping good brain-*builders*, which build better
brains.

## Phase map

- **Phase 1 (done, verified):** prove evolvable trinary-logic tissue can
  learn anything (addition demo). Open-sourced.
- **Phase 2 (now):** toy tasks → real tasks (market direction), public
  scoring, compounding weekly structures.
- **Phase 3 (the bet):** working intelligences as smart contracts (execution
  burns QUs); oracles = observation, outsourced compute = action.
  Intelligence as on-chain economy.

The product was never "an AI." It's a breeding program for AIs, and the
archive is its memory.
