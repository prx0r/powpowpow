# QUBIC bull vs bear — full objective case (2026-09-26)

Valuation anchor (measured, warehouse price_history + official supply):
- Price: $4.08e-07 (2026-09-23 close)
- Circulating mcap (~138T): **~$56M**
- Fully-diluted (200T cap): **~$82M**
- Net issuance: 32.14B QU/day ≈ **$13.1k/day, ~$4.8M/yr ≈ 8.5%/yr dilution**
- Drawdown from Dec-2024 peak ($4.72e-06): **-91.4%**

## The bull case

1. **Tiny absolute overhang.** $13k/day clears on one motivated buyer.
   At $56M mcap, even modest sustained demand moves price hard —
   asymmetry favors upside *if* demand appears.
2. **Schedule only tightens.** Yearly halvings (279 next, ~Aug 2027),
   burn ratcheting (55% → 77.5% → higher). Every future epoch issues
   less net supply than the last. Time is structurally long.
3. **Burns compound the story.** 41.5T+ burned to date; Supply Watcher
   can push realized burn above schedule. At some point gross burns
   exceed net issuance and supply peaks (~196.8T projected) then declines.
4. **Capitulation may be done.** -91.4% with weakening sell pressure as
   issuance value collapses; remaining sellers are increasingly
   price-insensitive holders, not forced miner flow.
5. **Unpriced optionality.** Any single item — working AI fee revenue,
   major exchange liquidity, oracle demand, Aigarth milestone — reprices
   a $56M asset violently. The market prices none of it today.
6. **Verified infrastructure discount.** Real weekly training loop,
   published research, live mainnet pillars — versus typical $56M
   altcoins with none of the above.

## The bear case

1. **Burden is live, daily, measured.** $13.1k/day of fresh supply vs
   ~$4k top-20 bid depth ≈ 3x burden. Books cannot absorb it; sellers
   discount; that discount IS the -91.4%.
2. **Burns are monetary, not usage.** Scheduled burn cuts new supply —
   it proves nothing about demand. Conflating the two is the bull trap.
   No fee-burn revenue exists yet at any scale.
3. **~60T QU still to come.** Circulating (~138T) → cap (200T) leaves
   years of future issuance at any price. Fully-diluted buyers pay $82M
   for the privilege of funding it.
4. **Miners must sell; buyers merely might.** Computors run real hardware
   with real bills — supply is scheduled, demand is discretionary. That
   asymmetry only resolves via usage or halving-driven scarcity, slowly.
5. **Alt market + execution risk.** 2025–26 alt bleed vs BTC; Aigarth
   milestones (extrapolation, candle task, products) all pending. Every
   one can fail or stall; price assumes none succeed, but also no floor
   if sentiment worsens.
6. **Peak was narrative, not value.** Dec-2024 $4.7e-6 priced AI hype +
   bull market, not cash flows (there were none then either). Nothing
   says that multiple returns without a new story backed by revenue.

## Net assessment

Bullish the *schedule* (tightening supply, tiny absolute overhang,
real optionality at $56M). Bearish everything on demand's side (no
usage revenue, daily measured burden, 60T still to issue). Asymmetric
bet with a supply fuse and no visible spark — the spark is the entire
trade. Watch: realized-vs-scheduled burn deviation, fee-burn revenue,
burden-vs-book trend, winning-architecture persistence.

## Addendum 2026-09-26 — September all-hands (Sep 17, published Sep 22)

### New bull evidence

1. **0.38 verified with trajectory.** 0.13 → 0.38 in ~3 months, doubling
   roughly every 6 weeks, CPU-only, <200 neurons, ahead of several
   frontier marks. Open-sourced after Kaggle closes (Nov 2) — verifiable,
   not trust-me. Trend matters more than level; trend is steep.
2. **Ant Colony live = retention solved structurally.** Training now
   compounds across epochs instead of resetting. Removes a core
   bear objection (throwaway weekly attempts).
3. **Three pillars converging.** Contracts + oracles (Feb) + outsourced
   compute (summer) ≈ complete. Crosschain oracle (read Ethereum
   directly, no bridge) unlocks trustless bridges + live market feeds.
   The "AI living on-chain" plumbing is mostly shipped, not planned.
4. **Live BTC prediction via oracle is next.** First task on live data,
   not static history — tests oracle + training + scoring together. The
   single highest-signal upcoming catalyst for the whole thesis.
5. **Real-world rails appearing.** AEON: 20M+ merchants, 6 countries;
   AlchemyPay: 173-country fiat on-ramp (first round tiny but measured:
   64 orders, $12.6k, 6,706 new addresses). MiCA groundwork for 450M EU
   consumers. Directionally all correct.
6. **Tokenomics proposal = free option.** Outside capital buys + burns,
   repaid 1.25x; zero cost if nobody signs. If it runs: simultaneous buy
   pressure + float shrink — a second demand leg independent of usage.
7. **Shipping pace.** ~307 commits / 20+ repos / 15+ contributors per
   month; Neuraxon 3.0 + 4th paper incoming; dev kit cut contract costs
   $10k → $0 (developer onboarding flywheel).

### New bear evidence / caveats

1. **Adoption is embryonic.** $12.6k across 38 buyers is a pilot, not
   traction. 115 wallets from a 5.8M-audience campaign is a rounding
   error. Rails exist; traffic doesn't — yet.
2. **Tokenomics proposal smells.** 1.25x repayment from what source?
   No-hedge covenants are nearly unenforceable; buy-then-repay loops
   read Ponzi-adjacent to every institution whose money they'd need.
   Feedback-stage for a reason — watch whether serious capital signs,
   because that IS the signal.
3. **0.38 is still 0.38.** Humans score 100%; field leader ~3%; most
   frontier models cluster 0.1–0.5%. The result is "best of the small"
   until it isn't. ARC-AGI-3 measures a narrow slice of reasoning, not
   general intelligence — don't let either side overclaim it.
4. **Everything new is unproven in production.** Ant Colony, crosschain
   oracle, outsourced compute all shipped within weeks. Shipped ≠
   battle-tested; expect incidents, and price them in.
5. **MiCA is slow by design.** Entity → whitepaper → submission →
   ramps/exchanges. EU access is quarters away, not weeks.
6. **Core bear math unchanged.** $13.1k/day burden, 8.5%/yr dilution,
   60T still to issue, zero AI revenue. Nothing in September reduced
   the daily sell pressure by a single dollar.

### Revised net

The bull case graduated from "schedule + hope" to "schedule + measured
research velocity + converging plumbing." The bear case is unchanged in
structure but now has a clock problem: each quarter of compounding
results without price response either builds coiled-spring tension or
proves the market doesn't care about any of it. The discriminating
variables for Q4: live BTC prediction numbers, tokenomics pilot uptake,
burden-vs-book trend. Everything else is commentary.
